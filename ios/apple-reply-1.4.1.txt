Hello App Review Team,

Thank you for the detailed feedback regarding citations for medical
information. We have addressed the issue in Build 1.0 (2) which is
now uploaded and ready for review.

WHAT WE CHANGED
---------------

We added a new prominent, always-visible "Sources of Medical
Information" section to every health-related calculator in the app.
Each source is shown as a clearly numbered card with:
  - the full title and the authoring organization,
  - a direct clickable link (opens in the system browser),
  - a one-line description of what the source supports (formula,
    classification ranges, recommendations, etc.).

The section is rendered immediately above the FAQ section and is NOT
collapsed by default — the user can see the citations without any
extra tap or scroll discovery. We also added an explicit disclaimer
that the calculator is informational and does not replace consultation
with a qualified medical professional.

WHICH CALCULATORS ARE AFFECTED
------------------------------

Three calculators in the "Health" category include medical information
and now carry full citations:

1. BMI / Body Mass Index Calculator
   URL: https://calk.kz/calculator/bmi/
   Sources cited:
     - World Health Organization — A healthy lifestyle: BMI classification
     - CDC — About Adult BMI
     - NIH/NHLBI — Clinical Guidelines on Overweight and Obesity in Adults
     - NIH NLM PMC10484485 — peer-reviewed BMI considerations

2. Daily Calories / BMR Calculator
   URL: https://calk.kz/calculator/calories/
   Sources cited:
     - Mifflin MD, St Jeor ST et al. (1990) — original peer-reviewed
       publication of the BMR equation (Am J Clin Nutr, PubMed 2305711)
     - Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics — position paper on energy
       estimation accuracy
     - USDA — Dietary Guidelines for Americans 2020-2025
     - WHO — Healthy diet fact sheet
     - Institute of Medicine — DRI for Energy, Macronutrients (PAL
       coefficients, AMDR ranges)

3. Pregnancy Calculator
   URL: https://calk.kz/calculator/pregnancy/
   Sources cited:
     - ACOG Committee Opinion No. 700 — Methods for Estimating the
       Due Date (the source of the Naegele's rule used in the calc)
     - WHO — Recommendations on antenatal care for a positive
       pregnancy experience
     - NIH NICHD — Pregnancy Information
     - Mayo Clinic — Fetal development (week-by-week)
     - Ministry of Health of Kazakhstan (РЦРЗ МЗ РК) — clinical
       protocols for physiological pregnancy

ADDITIONAL CHANGES
------------------

- The pre-existing FAQ "Official Sources" footer was also expanded
  from 1-2 generic links to 3-4 authoritative links per calculator
  (intentional duplication so sources appear in both the prominent
  block and the FAQ footer).
- A disclaimer note ("informational only, does not replace medical
  advice") is included inside every prominent block.

These changes are part of Build 1.0 (2). The website (calk.kz) and
the iOS WebView both render the same content, so reviewers can verify
on either platform.

If anything else is needed, we are happy to address it quickly.
