Hello App Review Team,

Build 1.0 (4) is now uploaded with significant additions that strengthen
the app and address all previous feedback.

WHAT'S NEW IN BUILD (4)
-----------------------

1. MEDICAL CITATIONS (Guideline 1.4.1) — ADDRESSED IN BUILD (3)
   All health calculators (BMI, Calories, Pregnancy, Body Fat, Sleep,
   Water Intake) now have prominent "Sources of Medical Information"
   blocks with authoritative citations to:
   - WHO, CDC, NIH/NHLBI, ACOG
   - Peer-reviewed papers: Mifflin-St Jeor (1990), Hodgdon & Beckett
     (1984), Hirshkowitz et al. (2015)
   - IOM Dietary Reference Intakes 2005
   - Ministry of Health of Kazakhstan clinical protocols

   The citation blocks are non-collapsible, with direct clickable links
   and disclaimer that calculations are informational only.

2. CONTENT EXPANSION (61 NEW CALCULATORS)
   We added 61 calculators that were promised in the App Store
   description but were not yet active. The app now offers 121 working
   calculators across 14 categories, fully matching the description:

   • Taxes (17): NEW corporate income tax (KPN), crypto tax, property
     sale tax, rental income tax, universal declaration (FNO 250/270),
     unified payment from payroll, tax regime comparison
   • Finance (15): NEW Otbasy Bank programs, FIRE, business ROI,
     break-even, margin/markup, cashback comparison, debt burden,
     inflation, cash flow gap, franchise payback
   • Auto (14): NEW KASKO insurance, vehicle TCO, auto leasing, car
     market value, car transfer, fuel cost, traffic fines, fancy plates,
     tire size
   • Legal (9): NEW inheritance, divorce, property division, statute of
     limitations, bankruptcy, moral damage
   • Social/Labor (12): NEW salary reverse, business trips, overtime,
     second job, average earnings, teacher salary
   • Construction (5) — NEW CATEGORY: concrete, brick, wallpaper,
     flooring, insulation
   • Real Estate (3) — NEW CATEGORY: fair rental price, apartment
     valuation, cost of living
   • Education (2) — NEW CATEGORY: ENT exam scores, GPA conversion
   • Religious (5): NEW Hajj/Umrah, Islamic mortgage
   • Health (6): all with medical citations
   • Tools/Converters (15): NEW age, pet age, Roman numerals, unit
     converter, timezone, password generator, QR code generator

3. UPDATED SCREENSHOTS
   We have uploaded new screenshots showing real working calculators
   (previously the Corporate Income Tax and ENT Score screenshots
   showed 404 pages because those calculators existed in code but were
   not yet registered with routes). All screenshots now show actual
   functional calculators.

4. OFFLINE COMPLIANCE
   QR Code Generator was refactored from an external API call
   (api.qrserver.com) to a fully offline JavaScript library (qrcode npm
   package). All 121 calculators now work completely offline.

5. SOURCE CITATIONS
   All medical/health calculators have authoritative sources. Tax and
   legal calculators reference specific articles of:
   - Tax Code of Republic of Kazakhstan (NK RK 2026)
   - Civil Code of RK (GK RK)
   - Labor Code of RK (TK RK)
   - Administrative Offenses Code (KoAP RK)
   - National Bank of Kazakhstan regulations

The app is a comprehensive, fully-offline calculator suite for
Kazakhstan residents, with no ads, no tracking, no sign-in, and no
personal data collection.

If you need any additional clarification or have specific concerns
about any calculator, please let us know.
